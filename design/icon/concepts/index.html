agent-kit icon concepts

Glyph and tile at 16, 32, 64 and 256 px, on light and on #0d1117. Tiles show a 22.4% platform mask.

1. Transcript

A cat-eared chat bubble with an apricot caret: the transcript, the kit's centre.

2. Stack

Cat-eared transcript panel over two context layers: transcript plus context and workspace.

3. Composer

A cat-eared prompt field with caret and a send button (Lucide arrow-up, ISC): the composer.

4. Kit

A 2x2 grid of widget tiles, one eared, one apricot: parts that compose into an app.
